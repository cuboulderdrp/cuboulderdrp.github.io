<h2 class="drp-section-title">Mentee responsibilities</h2>

<ol class="drp-responsibilities">
  <li>Meet with your mentor every week for at least one hour (excluding spring break) during the spring semester.</li>
  <li>Commit at least three hours weekly in total, including your mentor meeting.</li>
  <li>Attend an evening poster workshop with free dinner in the middle of the semester (date TBD; not on an exam day).</li>
  <li>Present your work at an evening poster session with free dinner near the end of the semester (date TBD; not on an exam day).</li>
  <li>Complete a short survey at the end of the program.</li>
</ol>

Mentors volunteer their time, and students should apply only if they can make these commitments. More details will be provided to students accepted into the program.

<h2 class="drp-section-title">Frequently asked questions</h2>

**Is previous research experience required?**

No. The program is intended to introduce undergraduate students to mathematical research. Specific course prerequisites differ by project.

**Do I need programming experience?**

Not for every project. Check the <a href="{{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}">proposed projects</a> for their individual requirements.

**Will I be assigned a specific topic?**

The posted projects provide starting points. You and your mentor may decide to develop the work in a different direction once you meet.

**When do we meet?**

Each mentor and their mentees arrange a weekly meeting of at least one hour during the spring semester, excluding spring break.

**What happens at the end of the semester?**

Students present their work at a poster session near the end of the semester.
