<p class="drp-eyebrow">University of Colorado Boulder · Applied Mathematics · Spring 2027</p>

<p class="drp-lede">The <strong>APPM Directed Reading Program (DRP)</strong> pairs graduate student mentors with 1–2 undergraduate students for a semester-long exploration of a mathematical topic.</p>

Projects may be theoretical, computational, or applied. They offer an introduction to research-level mathematical ideas at roughly the level of a slightly more challenging lower-division APPM course project. The program concludes with an undergraduate poster presentation at the end of the spring semester.
